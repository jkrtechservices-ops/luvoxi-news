The next home game is Tuesday, Nov. 10, against the Detroit Red Wings at 8 p.m. ET on TNT. After that, the club heads west to Utah for a Thursday road game. Nov. 7 Blues schedule in Nashville

The Blues travel to Bridgestone Arena in Nashville on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026. The puck drops at noon, giving fans a mid‑day showdown. Joel Hofer is projected to start in goal, while forward Mason McTavish will take warmups but remains a gametime decision. Coach has shifted Connor McMichael to center and Dalibor Dvorsky to wing for this matchup. These adjustments aim to boost offensive flow against the host team. The game offers a chance to see how the new line combinations perform before the upcoming home stretch.

Nov. 10 Blues schedule against Detroit Red Wings

Back in St. Louis, the Blues host the Detroit Red Wings on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, at Enterprise Center. The broadcast begins at 8 p.m. ET on TNT. Entering the game, the Blues sit at 7‑8‑2 and are 13th in the Western Conference with 16 points. The injury report lists Torey Krug out for the season (ankle), Tyler Tucker out (knee), Adam Jecho out (personal), and Dillon Dubé out (neck). Despite these absences, the club hopes to capitalize on home‑ice advantage and climb the standings.

Upcoming road trip to Utah

After the Detroit home game, the Blues continue their schedule with a road trip to Utah. The Thursday game following the Nov. 7 Nashville matchup will be against the Utah Mammoth. This marks the first away game after the Nashville visit. The travel schedule gives the team a chance to reset after two consecutive home games and test depth players in a different environment.

Potential return of Logan Mailloux

Logan Mailloux could be cleared to rejoin the lineup either on Tuesday, Nov. 10, when the Blues host Detroit, or on Thursday during the Utah road game. The timing depends on the league’s clearance process and his health status. If he returns on Nov. 10, he would provide a boost to the forward group that is missing several key players due to injuries. Coaches will weigh his readiness against the team’s immediate needs.

Lineup notes and forward roles

Beyond the specific adjustments for Nov. 7, the Blues have other forward assignments to watch. Aleksanteri Kaskimaki is expected to stay in a top‑6 forward role on Thursday in New Jersey, according to the Nov. 7 lineup preview. These roles indicate the coaching staff’s confidence in his two‑way play and scoring potential. Keeping an eye on how the line combinations evolve will help fans understand the team’s strategy as the schedule progresses.
