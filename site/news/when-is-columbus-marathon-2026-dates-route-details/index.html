Exact Dates of the 2026 Columbus Marathon

The Columbus Marathon weekend runs from Saturday, October 16 to Tuesday, October 18, 2026. The official kickoff is on Saturday, with the full marathon concluding on Tuesday at North Bank Park. As of October 2026, these dates are confirmed by local broadcasters 10TV and WSYX. Mark your calendar early, especially if you plan to watch the finish or volunteer, because the event spans three days of activity across the city.

Start and Finish Locations for 2026

Runners will line up at the start line on Saturday, October 16, with the course winding through downtown Columbus before looping back to finish at North Bank Park on Tuesday, October 18. North Bank Park, located along the Scioto River, serves as the final destination for the marathon, half‑marathon and other race divisions. The start location and finish line are both situated near major transit hubs, making it easy for spectators and participants to access public transportation.

Road Closures During the Marathon Weekend

City officials have announced road closures for October 16, 17 and 18, 2026. Major arteries in downtown Columbus, including portions of High Street and the Scioto Mile, will be closed to vehicle traffic while the race proceeds. Detour routes are posted on the Columbus Department of Transportation website, and signage will be in place the day before each closure. Residents and commuters should plan alternate routes or use public transit to avoid delays.

Runner Participation Numbers

The 2026 Columbus Marathon is projected to draw approximately 15,000 runners, a figure confirmed by WSYX and echoed across local media outlets. This sizable field is a blend of elite competitors, seasoned club athletes, and a wave of first‑time marathoners eager to experience the city’s newly unveiled course. Over the past decade, participant numbers have risen steadily, positioning the Columbus Marathon as one of the Midwest’s most sought‑after road races and a key fixture on the national marathon calendar.

The influx of runners translates into a bustling race‑week experience. An expanded marathon expo will open at the Ohio Expo Center on Friday, October 17, offering vendors, gear demos, and health‑screening stations to accommodate the larger crowd. Meanwhile, a volunteer network of more than 2,000 locals—students, retirees, and community groups—will staff water stations, course marshals, and finish‑line services across the three‑day road‑closure window (October 16‑18).

Finisher celebrations are set for Tuesday, October 18, at North Bank Park, where a post‑race festival will feature live music, food trucks, and a medal ceremony for all 15,000 participants. The scale of the event also means increased logistical coordination with the city: traffic detours, public‑transport adjustments, and enhanced safety patrols will be in place throughout downtown Columbus to ensure a smooth experience for runners and spectators alike.

New Course for the 2026 Marathon

The Columbus Dispatch reported that the 2026 marathon will follow a newly designed route. While the start and finish remain familiar, the middle sections have been adjusted to showcase different neighborhoods and improve runner flow. Organizers say the changes were made after extensive community feedback and aim to reduce congestion on popular streets. Runners can expect fresh scenery, new elevation points, and updated aid stations along the revised course.
