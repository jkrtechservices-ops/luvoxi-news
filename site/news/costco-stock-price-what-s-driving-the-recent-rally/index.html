What has driven Costco's recent stock price rally?

Costco’s rally is rooted in a streak of earnings surprises. Six straight beats have lifted the share price about 20% this year, according to a 247wallst.com report dated November 6, 2026. The company’s October net sales rose 12.6% year over year to $24.5 billion, reinforcing the earnings narrative. Analysts also highlighted comparable sales growth—11.1% overall and 7.3% when gas and currency effects are stripped out—signaling robust consumer demand across the warehouse chain.

How much has Costco stock increased so far this year?

As of early November 2026, Costco shares have risen roughly 20% year‑to‑date. The 247wallst.com article notes six consecutive earnings beats contributed to this gain. CNBC reported that the stock had climbed more than 19% since bottoming out in mid‑September, confirming a strong upward trajectory within a short period.

What is Costco's current price relative to its May record high?

On Thursday, November 5, 2026, Costco Wholesale Corp. (NASDAQ: COST) rallied close to 2 percent, nudging the share price to just under its May record high of $1,094. That level, first set in early May, now sits only a few dollars away from the current market price, underscoring the strength of the momentum that has carried the stock higher over the past several months.

The climb is anchored in a string of six consecutive earnings beats, which have propelled Costco’s shares up roughly 20 percent year‑to‑date, according to a 247WallSt.com report published on November 6. Since bottoming out in mid‑September, the stock has surged more than 19 percent, a rebound driven by robust top‑line growth and resilient consumer demand. In October alone, Costco reported net sales of $24.5 billion—a 12.6 percent increase versus the same month a year earlier—and comparable sales rose 11.1 percent year over year. When gas sales and currency effects are stripped out, comparable sales still climbed 7.3 percent, a figure highlighted by Goldman Sachs analysts in the CNBC coverage of the earnings release.

While the price action is impressive, the valuation remains demanding. Costco’s trailing price‑to‑earnings multiple sits at about 49 times earnings, reflecting investors’ willingness to pay a premium for the retailer’s consistent performance and strong membership base. Nonetheless, the proximity to the $1,094 peak signals that the market still believes the company’s growth trajectory can justify such lofty multiples, at least in the near term.

What were Costco's October net sales and year‑over‑year growth?

Costco reported October net sales of $24.5 billion, a 12.6% increase from the same month a year earlier, according to CNBC on November 5, 2026. Comparable sales rose 11.1% year over year, while the figure excluding gas sales and currency fluctuations grew 7.3%, reflecting solid underlying demand.

What is Costco's trailing P/E ratio and why is it considered high?

The trailing price‑to‑earnings ratio for Costco stands at 49x, as noted by 247wallst.com on November 6, 2026. A P/E this high signals that investors are paying a premium for future growth, but it also raises concerns about valuation sustainability if earnings momentum slows.
