Zelda Switch 2 console launch date in the US

Nintendo has set the official US launch for the Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 on Thursday, October 29, 2026. The date appears on Nintendo’s product pages and aligns with the company’s anniversary celebration. As of October 2026, the console is ready for purchase at participating retailers. The launch coincides with a special edition carrying case and a Pro Controller, making it a bundled event for fans and collectors alike.

Price of the limited‑edition Zelda Switch 2 console

The Zelda Switch 2 console carries a price tag of $519.99, which is $20 more than the standard Switch 2 model. This premium reflects the anniversary branding, unique color scheme, and the inclusion of a custom carrying case. The price is listed on Nintendo’s official product page and is consistent across major US retailers. Buyers should expect the same cost whether they shop online or in‑store, though occasional retailer promotions could affect the final amount.

Because the console is a limited‑edition run, it may sell out quickly, so early purchase is advisable for those who want the official price without resale mark‑ups.

Availability of the Zelda Switch 2 Pro Controller

The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Pro Controller is slated for release on October 29, 2026, at a regular price of $89.99, according to Nintendo’s official listing. As of October 27, 2026, Target’s page shows the controller as “Out of Stock,” while Amazon UK lists it as “Temporarily out of stock.” Best Buy has a product page but no guarantee of units. Walmart is expected to stock both the console and the controller on launch day, making it the most reliable source for immediate purchase.

Which retailer is most likely to have Zelda Switch 2 stock on launch day

Walmart is the retailer most likely to have Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 consoles available on launch day, October 29, 2026. The company has confirmed stock for the console, Pro Controller, and carrying case. Other major chains such as Target and Best Buy have shown limited or uncertain inventory, with Target’s listing already marked “Out of Stock” as of October 27, 2026. Shoppers looking for guaranteed availability should prioritize Walmart, either online or at local stores.

Release date for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time on Switch 2

The classic title The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is scheduled to launch on the Zelda Switch 2 on November 5, 2026. This release follows the console’s debut by one week, giving early adopters time to set up their new hardware before the flagship game arrives. Nintendo has listed the date on its official product page, confirming the timeline for fans eager to revisit the beloved adventure on the upgraded platform.
