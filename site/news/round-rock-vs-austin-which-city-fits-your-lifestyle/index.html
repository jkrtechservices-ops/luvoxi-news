Round Rock vs Austin: Which City Fits Your Lifestyle?

By Luvoxi News Editorial Team · Nov 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Representative stock image via Pixabay, not a photo of the event.

ShareWhatsAppXFacebook

Round Rock and Austin sit side‑by‑side in Central Texas, but they offer very different living experiences. Round Rock is a midsize suburb with a strong tech presence and higher median incomes, while Austin is a large, cultural hub with higher home prices but a lower cost of rent relative to income. Understanding the numbers helps you choose the city that matches your budget and lifestyle.

Key takeaways
  • Round Rock: smaller, higher median income, lower home prices.
  • Austin: much larger, higher home values, similar rent costs.
  • Both cities have rent at about 21‑22% of household income.
  • Round Rock leans toward families; Austin attracts creatives.

Population and Size: How Big Is Each City?

Austin’s population stands at 979,539, making it the 12th‑most populous city in the United States. Round Rock is home to 127,786 residents, roughly 667% smaller than Austin. The size difference means Austin feels more urban, with a sprawling 305.1 square‑mile footprint, while Round Rock offers a tighter community feel within the Greater Austin metropolitan area.

For those who prefer a bustling city with diverse neighborhoods, Austin’s scale provides more options. If you value a smaller, more navigable environment, Round Rock’s modest size may be more appealing.

Income Levels: Which City Pays More?

The median household income in Round Rock is $99,287 per year, about $5,629 (6%) higher than Austin’s median of $93,658. Higher incomes in Round Rock are driven in part by the presence of Dell’s international headquarters, which employs roughly 16,000 people locally. Austin’s income reflects its broader mix of tech, government, and creative industries.

Higher earnings can translate into more disposable income, but the cost of housing and other expenses also shape overall affordability.

Rent Costs: What Will You Pay Each Month?

Median gross rent in Round Rock is $1,763 per month, slightly higher—by $34 (2%)—than Austin’s $1,729. Both cities keep rent at roughly the same share of household income: about 21% in Round Rock and 22% in Austin. This similarity means that, despite Round Rock’s higher median income, renters will feel a comparable financial burden in both locations.

If you are looking for the lowest possible rent, the difference is marginal; other factors like commute length and amenities may carry more weight.

Home Values and Ownership Rates

The median value of owner‑occupied homes in Round Rock is $418,600, while Austin’s median home price is $555,300—$136,700 (33%) higher. Homeownership is also more common in Round Rock, with 56% of occupied housing units owner‑occupied versus 43% in Austin. These numbers suggest that buying a home is more attainable in Round Rock, especially for first‑time buyers.

Austin’s higher home values reflect its rapid growth and desirability, but they also mean a larger mortgage burden for buyers.

Affordability Snapshot: Cost of Living in Context

Both cities keep rent at about one‑fifth of median household income, a sign of balanced rental affordability. However, the larger gap in home values makes Round Rock more affordable for buyers, while Austin’s higher incomes offset its steeper housing costs for renters. When evaluating overall affordability, consider whether you plan to rent or buy, and how much you value homeownership.

Round Rock’s lower home prices and higher ownership rate favor families and long‑term residents. Austin’s higher incomes and cultural amenities may attract younger professionals willing to pay more for location.

What Each City Is Known For

Round Rock is best known for being the headquarters of Dell and for retail hubs like IKEA, Premium Outlets, and the La Frontera mixed‑use center. Its historic Old Town preserves early Texas architecture, and the city straddles the Balcones Escarpment, offering both prairie and hill country landscapes.

Austin, the state capital, is famous for its live‑music scene, tech startups, and outdoor recreation on Lady Bird Lake and the Colorado River. The city’s cultural reputation draws artists, entrepreneurs, and a steady stream of newcomers seeking a vibrant urban lifestyle.

Who Thrives Best in Each City

Round Rock suits families and professionals who value a smaller community, higher median incomes, and a greater chance of homeownership. Its proximity to Austin (about 15 miles north) also offers easy access to the larger city’s job market without the daily congestion.

Austin appeals to creatives, tech workers, and those who thrive on a larger, culturally rich environment. The city’s extensive music venues, festivals, and outdoor spaces make it ideal for singles and young couples who prioritize lifestyle experiences over lower housing costs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Round Rock or Austin cheaper to live in?

Round Rock is generally cheaper for homebuyers because its median home value is $418,600 versus Austin’s $555,300. Rent is similar, with Round Rock at $1,763 and Austin at $1,729 per month.

Which has higher incomes, Round Rock or Austin?

Round Rock has a higher median household income at $99,287, which is about $5,629 (6%) more than Austin’s median of $93,658.

Is rent higher in Round Rock or Austin?

Rent is slightly higher in Round Rock at $1,763 per month, compared with $1,729 in Austin—a difference of $34 (2%).

Which is better for families, Round Rock or Austin?

Round Rock tends to be better for families because it offers a smaller community, higher homeownership rate (56% vs 43%), and more affordable home prices.

Is Round Rock or Austin bigger?

Austin is much larger, with a population of 979,539, while Round Rock has 127,786 residents—about 667% smaller than Austin.

The bottom line

Round Rock delivers higher incomes, lower home prices, and a family‑friendly vibe, while Austin offers a massive cultural scene and higher home values but similar rent costs. Match your budget, lifestyle preferences, and long‑term goals to the city that aligns best with your needs.
