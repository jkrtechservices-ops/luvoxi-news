Katy vs San Antonio: Which City Is Right for You?

By Luvoxi News Editorial Team · Nov 07, 2026 · 4 min read

Katy and San Antonio offer very different living experiences. Katy is a small, affluent suburb of Houston with high incomes and home ownership, while San Antonio is a large, historic city with lower costs and a diverse urban vibe. Understanding the numbers—population, earnings, rent, and housing—helps you match a city to your budget, family needs, and personal preferences.

Key takeaways
  • Katy’s median income is $49,856 higher than San Antonio’s.
  • Home values in Katy average $166,000 more than in San Antonio.
  • San Antonio’s rent is 24% of household income versus 17% in Katy.
  • San Antonio’s population is over 1.4 million; Katy’s is just 25,184.

Population and Land Area: Size Matters

San Antonio is a major metropolis with an estimated population of 1,479,835, making it the seventh‑most populous city in the United States. It spreads across 465.4 square miles, offering a wide range of neighborhoods, cultural districts, and commercial zones. By contrast, Katy is a much smaller community of 25,184 residents, covering 15.312 square miles. Its compact size creates a more suburban feel, with most amenities clustered near the historic downtown and along Interstate 10. If you prefer a bustling urban environment with many options, San Antonio’s scale is a clear advantage. If you favor a close‑knit community where you likely know many neighbors, Katy’s modest size may suit you better.

Income Levels: Who Earns More?

The median household income in Katy stands at $114,912, which is $49,856, or about 77%, higher than San Antonio’s median of $65,056. This income gap reflects Katy’s reputation as an affluent suburb with many professional families, while San Antonio’s earnings are more modest, reflecting a broader mix of occupations and industries. Higher incomes in Katy often translate into greater discretionary spending power, but they also correlate with higher housing costs. San Antonio’s lower median income can be appealing for those seeking a more affordable lifestyle, especially if you are comfortable with a tighter budget.

Rent Costs and Affordability

Rent in Katy has a median gross price of $1,602 per month, which is $278, or about 21%, higher than San Antonio’s median rent of $1,324. However, because Katy’s median household income is much higher, rent represents roughly 17% of income, compared with 24% in San Antonio. Financial experts often view rent under 30% of income as affordable, so both cities fall well within that range. For renters, San Antonio offers a slightly lower monthly bill, but the higher income in Katy means renters there may feel less financial strain relative to their earnings.

Home Values and Ownership Rates

The median value of owner‑occupied homes in Katy is $401,700, which is $166,000, or about 70%, higher than San Antonio’s median home value of $235,700. Homeownership is also more common in Katy, with about 81% of occupied housing units owned, versus 52% in San Antonio. This suggests that Katy attracts buyers who can afford higher‑priced homes and prefer long‑term stability. In San Antonio, a larger share of renters and a lower home price point make it easier for first‑time buyers or those with tighter budgets to enter the market.

What Each City Is Known For

Katy is best known for its suburban charm, proximity to Houston, and a history rooted in the Missouri–Kansas–Texas Railroad. Its location at the tripoint of Harris, Fort Bend, and Waller counties gives residents easy access to major highways and the amenities of Greater Houston while retaining a small‑town feel. San Antonio, on the other hand, is celebrated for its historic missions, Spanish colonial heritage, and a vibrant cultural scene that includes the River Walk, military installations, and a strong tourism industry. The city’s size and history attract visitors and residents who enjoy a blend of tradition and modern growth.

Who Thrives Best in Each City

Katy suits families and professionals who prioritize high household income, strong home‑ownership rates, and a suburban environment with easy commutes to Houston’s job market. The median age of 37.6 years indicates a mature, family‑oriented population. San Antonio appeals to a broader demographic, including young adults, military personnel, and families seeking lower housing costs and a rich cultural backdrop. Its median age of 34.9 years points to a slightly younger population, and the larger rental market offers flexibility for those not ready to buy. Both cities provide distinct lifestyles, so aligning your career, family stage, and cultural preferences with these traits will guide the right choice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Katy or San Antonio cheaper to live in?

San Antonio is generally cheaper. Its median rent is $1,324 versus $1,602 in Katy, and the median home value is $235,700 compared with $401,700 in Katy. However, Katy’s higher median income makes housing costs a smaller share of earnings.

Which has higher incomes, Katy or San Antonio?

Katy has the higher median household income at $114,912, which is $49,856 more than San Antonio’s median of $65,056.

Is rent higher in Katy or San Antonio?

Rent is higher in Katy at $1,602 per month, compared with $1,324 in San Antonio.

Which is better for families, Katy or San Antonio?

Katy often appeals to families seeking higher incomes, higher home‑ownership rates, and a suburban setting. San Antonio offers more affordable housing and a wider range of cultural activities, which can also be attractive for families on a tighter budget.

Is Katy or San Antonio bigger?

San Antonio is far larger, with a population of 1,479,835, while Katy’s population is 25,184. San Antonio’s land area is also much greater at 465.4 square miles versus Katy’s 15.312 square miles.

The bottom line

Katy delivers higher earnings, higher home values, and a suburban feel, making it ideal for affluent families and commuters to Houston. San Antonio provides a much larger, culturally rich urban environment with lower costs and more rental options, appealing to a diverse range of residents. Choose the city that aligns with your income, housing preferences, and lifestyle priorities.
