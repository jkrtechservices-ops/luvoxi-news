What Does the Median Income Figure Mean for Chandler Residents?

The median household income of $108,095 represents the middle point of all earnings reported in Chandler. Half of the households earn more, and half earn less. This figure reflects the combined incomes of all earners in a household, including wages, salaries, and other sources. For families, it often translates into a comfortable ability to cover basic expenses, save for the future, and afford discretionary spending. Compared with national averages, Chandler's median income suggests a relatively affluent community, which influences local businesses, services, and overall economic health.

How Housing Costs Relate to Median Income in Chandler

Housing is the largest expense for most households. In Chandler, the median gross rent is $1,902 per month, which totals $22,824 annually. With 65% of occupied housing units owner‑occupied, many residents invest in long‑term equity. When comparing rent or mortgage obligations to the median income of $108,095, housing typically consumes a manageable portion of earnings, though individual budgets will vary based on family size and lifestyle choices.

Age Demographics and Income Potential

Understanding the age distribution, therefore, is essential for maintaining Chandler’s robust median income trajectory.
