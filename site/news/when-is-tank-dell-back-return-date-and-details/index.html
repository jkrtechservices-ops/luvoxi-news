After the surgery, Dell embarked on a grueling rehabilitation program that spanned more than a year, incorporating progressive strength training, proprioception drills, and sport‑specific conditioning. The Texans’ medical staff monitored his progress closely, and only after he successfully completed a two‑week, full‑speed practice stint in mid‑October 2026 did they deem him ready for game action.
