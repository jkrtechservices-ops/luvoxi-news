Editorial Policy

How articles are made

Topics come from public trend data and a fixed list of city guide subjects. We research each topic with search tools, then write the article with AI assistance and run automated checks on length, structure and sensitive content. We aim to state only facts supported by published sources.

Topics we avoid

To reduce the risk of errors and harm we do not cover crimes, accidents, deaths, missing people, minors, health claims, elections or politics, or ongoing emergencies.
